## Steps
Suggested worked examples (results must be exact or within the stated tolerance):
1. **random-walks** — checks: variance of a 100-step ±1 walk (100); choice: scaling that gives a Brownian limit
   (steps ±√(1/n)). Worked: P(S₄ = 2) for a symmetric walk (4·(1/2)⁴ = 0.25), mean and variance of S_n.
2. **probability-spaces** — worked: the σ-algebra generated by one event on a 4-point space (count: 4 sets), then
   a random variable's expectation under a given measure.
3. **filtrations** — worked: on the two-coin tree, E[X | F₁] in both branches, then E[E[X | F₁]] = E[X].
4. **brownian-properties** — worked: Cov(W_s, W_t) = min(s, t) for s = 0.5, t = 2 (0.5); Var of W_2 − W_0.5 (1.5);
   the scaled process (1/c)W_{c²t} variance check.
5. **reflection-principle** — worked: P(max_{t≤1} W_t ≥ 1) = 2(1 − Φ(1)) = 0.3173; then P(τ_1 ≤ 1).
6. **brownian-bridge** — worked: mean and variance of the bridge at t = 0.25 with T = 1, endpoint b = 1
   (0.25 and 0.1875).
7. **ito-integral** — worked: E[∫₀ᵀ W_t dW_t] = 0 and its variance by the isometry (T²/2 at T = 2 → 2); then
   ∫ W dW = (W_T² − T)/2 at a sample W_T.
8. **feynman-kac** — worked: for u(t, x) solving the heat equation with terminal x², u(0, x) = x² + T at x = 1,
   T = 0.5 (1.5); compare with the lesson's Monte Carlo figure default.
9. **mean-reversion** — worked: OU mean and variance at t = 1 from the lesson's default parameters (κ, θ, σ),
   and the half-life ln 2 / κ (already a check id `mean-reversion/half-life`; the worked step may reuse the value).
10. **jump-diffusion** — checks: expected number of jumps in a year with λ = 2 (2); choice: effect of jumps on
    kurtosis (raises it). Worked: total variance σ² + λ(μ_J² + σ_J²) with the figure's defaults, then the
    diffusion share.

Compute, paste, write HU, run tests, shrink allowlists.

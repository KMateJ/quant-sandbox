# 05 — Market making lab: live quoting rounds

Status: done (2026-10-28, opus-main) · Depends on: 01, 02 · Suggested model: Sonnet · Est. size: L

## Why
This is the area's flagship lab and the module that later hosts the desk. The reader *is* the market
maker for 60-second rounds: they drag a bid and an ask around a moving fair value while customers arrive;
tight quotes fill often but earn little, wide quotes earn a lot per fill but rarely fill, and some
customers know where the price is going. The "spread vs income" curve is not shown up front: it is built
from the reader's own rounds, and only then compared with the theory.

## Goal
1. A real-time **quote rail**: fair value scrolling left, your bid/ask as draggable lines, customers arriving
   as markers that either hit your quote (fill, "+2¢") or fade away; inventory gauge and live P&L.
2. Rounds: each finished round adds a dot to "your rounds" (average half-spread vs P&L per minute); "Show
   theory" overlays the expected-income curve and its optimum; "Auto" runs 40 bot rounds at a chosen spread.
3. Replay: after a round, informed customers' fills are revealed with their 3-second markout.
4. Predict (`mkm-best-spread`) and Challenge (`mkm-round`).

## Non-goals
- No limit order book here (that is plans 03–04 and the desk); no inventory-optimal quoting (plan 06).

## Constraints
- README §2 and §4 lab recipe. Prices in cents relative to the start fair value; P&L in $ for 100-share fills.

## Steps
1. **Model** (`marketMaking.types.ts`, `round.math.ts`): params `{ lambda: number /* arrivals/s */, k: number
   /* 1/¢ */, informed: number, sigma: number /* ¢/√s */, horizon: number /* s, informed look-ahead */,
   imbalance: number /* −0.5…0.5, noise buy share − 0.5 */, lot: number /* 100 */ }`, defaults `{ lambda: 4,
   k: 0.5, informed: 0, sigma: 1, horizon: 3, imbalance: 0, lot: 100 }`. A round is 60 s in steps of 0.1 s.
   `roundSchedule(params, seed)` pre-draws everything: fair path (600 steps + horizon) and arrivals
   `{ t, informed: boolean, buy: boolean, premium: number /* Exp(k) ¢ */ }` (Poisson in each step). `stepRound(
   state, quotes: { bid: number; ask: number; live: boolean }, schedule)` processes arrivals in the step:
   a noise buyer trades at your ask if `ask − fair ≤ premium` (seller: `fair − bid ≤ premium`); an informed
   customer buys if `fair[t + horizon] > ask`, sells if `< bid`. Each fill updates a `Ledger` (via
   `applyFill`, mid = fair at fill, fee 0) and appends `{ t, side, price, informed, markout }` (markout =
   side-signed fair move over `horizon`). Quotes are an **input per step**, so the same seed with different
   play gives the same customers.
2. **Theory** — shared with the guide lesson "Adverse selection and informed trading"
   (`docs/plans/market-making-guides/05-adverse-selection.md`), so it lives in **`src/lib/market/informed.ts`**.
   If that file exists, import it unchanged; otherwise create it with exactly these exports (and append a
   `CONTRACT` line to this LOG and to `docs/plans/market-making-guides/LOG.md`):
   `informedLoss(delta, s) = s·normPdf(delta/s) − delta·(1 − normCdf(delta/s))` (expected loss to one informed
   arrival per side); `incomeRate(delta, p: { lambda; k; informed; sigma; horizon })` per second for symmetric
   half-spread δ = `λ·[(1 − π)·δ·e^{−kδ} − 2π·informedLoss(δ, σ√horizon)]` (for π = 0 it must equal
   `expectedSpreadIncome(δ, λ/2, k)`); `bestDelta(p)` (golden-section on [0, 6/k]); `breakEvenDelta(p)`
   (smallest δ with income ≥ 0, via `bisection` in `src/lib/math/roots.ts`). The lab's own `theory.math.ts` only
   adapts these to the lab's params and units.
3. **Bots and summaries** (`rounds.math.ts`): `roundSummary(state)` → `{ avgHalfSpread (time-weighted),
   pnlPerMin, fills, informedFills, maxAbsInventory, parts: decompose(...) }`; `runBotRounds(p, δ, seed, n =
   40)` (symmetric bot at δ around fair, never skews) → summaries. Runs on the main thread (40 × 600 steps).
4. **Hooks**: `useQuoteRound.ts` (reducer: `start`, `step`, `setQuotes`, `pull`, `finish`; `useTicker(running,
   100, step)`; reduced motion → "Step 1 s" button and no floating labels), `useMarketMakingLab.ts` (mode,
   params from `marketMaking.url.ts`, rounds list in state for the session, theory toggle, bot runs, predict
   and challenge state).
5. **Quote rail** (`components/QuoteRail.tsx` + `RailQuotes.tsx` + `RailCustomers.tsx`): SVG, x = last 20 s,
   y = ¢ around fair (auto range ±(3/k + 2σ)). Fair value line; bid line `var(--positive)`, ask line
   `var(--negative)`, both with square drag handles at the right edge (`pointerdown` on handle, vertical drag,
   0.5¢ snap); the band between them lightly tinted. Each arrival appears at the right edge at the price the
   customer would accept (`fair ± premium`; informed drawn the same as noise during play), travels 1 s to
   the quote; a fill shows a small "+2.0¢" or "−1.5¢" label that fades (no label under reduced motion).
   Keyboard (rail focused): ↑/↓ move both quotes 0.5¢ (skew), ←/→ narrow/widen 0.5¢, P pull/restore,
   Space start/pause. Visible key legend under the rail.
6. **HUD** (`RoundHud.tsx`, `InventoryGauge.tsx`): timer, P&L (sign), fills (buys · sells), inventory gauge
   (horizontal, square, centre zero, ±limit ticks, colour + "long"/"short" word), live split
   spread / adverse / inventory from `decompose`.
7. **After the round** (`RoundSummary.tsx`, `ReplayStrip.tsx`): stats; a replay of the 60 s timeline with
   informed fills marked by a different shape (triangle vs circle, plus legend) and each fill's markout as a
   short vertical segment; one-line explanation keyed on the result ("Informed customers took 6 of your 21
   fills and cost you $4.10").
8. **Your rounds** (`RoundsScatter.tsx`, `BotRuns.tsx`): `ScatterChart` of rounds (x avg half-spread ¢,
   y P&L/min $), different marker per informed setting; "Show theory" (enabled after 3 rounds or in Predict)
   draws `incomeRate × 60 × lot/100` and marks `bestDelta` and `breakEvenDelta`; "Auto" (δ stepper) adds 40
   bot dots as a faint cloud plus their mean ± 1 sd and a `Histogram` of bot P&L.
9. **Predict** (`mkm-best-spread`, kind place): theory hidden; the reader clicks the x-axis of the rounds chart
   to place the half-spread they think maximises income; `usePredictReveal({ truth: bestDelta(p), tolerance:
   0.4, resetKey: params, challengeId: "mkm-best-spread" })`, `PredictReveal input="plot"`. Two
   `PredictExplain` items: "Double the spread from 2¢ to 4¢: does income per minute double?" (no: fills fall by
   e^{−2k}) and "Informed share goes from 0% to 30%: quote tighter or wider?" (wider: the best spread moves out
   and there is a break-even spread below which every fill loses on average).
10. **Challenge** (`mkm-round`, kind score): informed 20%, imbalance 0; hit = round P&L ≥ target and |inventory|
    ≤ 1,000 shares throughout; target = 60% of `incomeRate(bestDelta) × 60 × lot/100` (calibrate: the bot at
    `bestDelta` should hit on ~40–60% of seeds 1–20; record). `const CHALLENGE = "mkm-round"`,
    `recordChallenge(CHALLENGE, hit, Date.now())`, `ChallengeRecord`.
11. **View** (`MarketMakingView.tsx`): `PageHeader` actions = mode `SegmentedControl` (Simulate · Predict ·
    Challenge), `SeedControl compact`, New round; `Workspace columns="sidebar"`: left `Panel` with flow
    controls (λ, k, π, σ, imbalance as `SliderField`s — secondary) and the formula block (income ≈ spread ×
    matched volume); main column: rail (dominant), HUD, then rounds chart + summary/replay. Phone: rail full
    width (min height 260px), drag handles ≥ 32px.
12. **Wiring**: append `mkm-best-spread` (place, "±0.4¢") and `mkm-round` (score, "≥ 60% of theory") rows;
    `sbxCh_mkm-best-spread` "Income-maximising spread" / HU "Bevételt maximalizáló spread" and `sbxCh_mkm-round`
    "Quoting round" / HU "Árjegyzési kör"; registry `status: "beta"`.
13. **Tests** `scripts/micro-market-making.test.mjs`: schedule determinism and independence from quotes;
    noise fill probability ≈ e^{−kδ} over 20,000 arrivals (±2%); `incomeRate` with π = 0 equals
    `expectedSpreadIncome(δ, λ/2, k)`; `bestDelta` = 1/k at π = 0 and increases with π; Monte Carlo of
    `runBotRounds` mean P&L within 3 standard errors of theory at δ = 1/k, π = 0.2; `decompose` identity on a
    round.

## Pitfalls
- **Per-step quotes, pre-drawn customers:** never draw randomness inside `stepRound` from a live RNG, or the
  bot comparison and replays break.
- **Render budget:** 10 updates/s; keep markers to the last 20 s (≤ ~100 elements), memoise static layers.
- **Pointer capture** on the drag handles (`setPointerCapture`) so fast drags don't drop; `touch-action: none`
  on the rail only.
- **The theory is for symmetric quotes and ignores inventory**; say so in the overlay legend.
- Don't edit `MarketMakingPages.tsx` (plan 11 owns the desk route).

About this tool

Narrative guides that connect the interactive tools into a story: financial instruments and their payoffs, the no-arbitrage principle, risk-neutral pricing, delta hedging and the road to the Black–Scholes formula. Each guide links to the labs where you can try the ideas yourself.

Frequently asked questions

In what order should I read the guides?

Start with instruments and no-arbitrage, then risk-neutral pricing and delta hedging; together they lead to the Black–Scholes formula.

Who are the guides for?

Students of financial and insurance mathematics and anyone who wants intuition behind derivatives pricing, not just the formulas.
